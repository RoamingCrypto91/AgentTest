What the model actually assumes

Four rules, and nothing else. One: most people who arrive are deciding whether this place is for them, and they decide fast. Two: a first post that nobody answers is the cheapest way to lose somebody — they conclude, correctly, that nobody was waiting for them. Three: a tie to another member is what makes leaving expensive; a tie to the host only makes it expensive to leave the host. Four: attention divides. Nine rooms is nine places to be ignored in.

Everything you can see on the field comes out of those four. Nobody programmed the ring that forms around you when members never answer each other, or the clump that forms when they do. Those are shapes the rules make.

This is a simulation, not anyone's data. It is worth what its assumptions are worth, and the assumptions are listed above so you can argue with them. The useful bit is that the four dials are the four things you actually control.
